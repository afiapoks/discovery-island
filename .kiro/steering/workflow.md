# Workflow and learning

- Work on the requested task only, then stop and report the result.
- Use one agent. Do not delegate or start unattended multi-agent build loops.
- Give Afia plain explanations of what changed and why the code works.
- Preserve the agreed idea and scope; ask only when a material decision is missing.
- Account for Kiro credits at the beginning and end of a session when visible.
- Credit grants pay for development assistance, not deployed AI inference.
- Do not treat grant approval as confirmed until a code is received and redeemed.
- Keep .kiro committed at the repository root and out of .gitignore.
- Keep builds, dependencies, secrets, and private account information out of Git.
- Never commit Kiro user IDs, credit codes, government IDs, or private screenshots.
- Starter planning files were prepared by Codex. Record actual Kiro actions separately.
- Update docs/BUILD-LOG.md with tool, task, files, checks, and credit use if known.
- Mark tasks complete only after their stated acceptance checks pass.
- Report a failed, interrupted, or unrun check accurately.
- Never invent citations, test output, deployment URLs, or hook execution.
- Do not deploy, enable paid inference, or publish social posts as part of setup.
