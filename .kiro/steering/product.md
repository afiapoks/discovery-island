# Product

- Working name: Discovery Island. Category: Resurrection, Kiroween 2026.
- Revive Encarta Kids' visual exploration of connected knowledge with new code.
- First audience: children roughly ages 7 to 12, with a chosen reading level.
- First world: Space. Include Earth, Moon, Sun, two experiments, one learning game.
- The modern addition is an AI guide grounded in a curated reference library.
- Connect questions to existing, checked activities. AI does not generate code.
- Make the scene feel like a world to explore, rather than a generic chatbot.
- Use original illustrations or assets with verified licenses and attribution.
- No additional worlds, accounts, leaderboards, or subscriptions in the first build.
- Present day/night and Moon phases accurately. Earth shadow is not the normal
  cause of Moon phases. Mark diagram distances and sizes as not to scale.
- Explain diagrams and controls in simple words. Avoid jargon and em dashes.
- Keep keyboard access, visible focus, readable text, and reduced motion support.
- A complete demo is: explore, ask a question, try an experiment, answer a quiz.
