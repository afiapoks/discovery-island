## Each approved content entry

- Stable passage ID and topic.
- Original explanation text and the factual claim it supports.
- Source title, publisher, exact URL, and date checked.
- Reviewer and review outcome.
- Applicable content/asset license and required attribution, if material is reused.

The AI guide can retrieve only reviewed entries. Source links should appear in
the app, and a human must check representative answers for factual correctness.
Do not invent a source URL, review date, or approval to fill a missing field.
