## Model limits

Day/night is a simplified equator model viewed from above the North Pole, with
sunlight fixed on the right. It does not calculate local sunrise times or seasons.
Moon phases use an ideal circular orbit and a northern Earth-view inset. The
orbit overview does not simulate eclipses or orbital tilt. All sizes and distances
are illustrative, not to scale. These limits are stated next to the controls.

The current guide returns prepared explanations from a small library. A keyword
match indicates a related explanation, not proof that any arbitrary question has
been answered. Unsupported questions have a scope message. Source links alone
are not proof of correctness, and the future AI guide needs factual review too.
